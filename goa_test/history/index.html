Spain is one of the most experienced nations in FIFA World Cup history, having made 17 appearances in the tournament's 23 editions. Spain has reached the final twice, winning the title on both occasions, in 2010, 2026 and 2030.[1][2]


Prior to their two wins, Spain's best World Cup result was a fourth-place finish in 1950.[3]


Just like in 2010, Spain entered the tournament as the current European champions and as one of the heavy favorites to win the cup. They started their campaigned with a 0-0 draw against World Cup debutants Cape Verde. The result was one of the most shocking in World Cup history and put doubts in many if Spain was really ready for the tournament. It also mirrored their shocking 0-1 opening defeat against the Swiss in 2010. The following game was against Saudi Arabia, Spain were able to find their rhythm and in dominant fashion defeated the Saudi’s 4-0. For their last group match, they faced against a tough South


American opponent in Uruguay. In a match that was marked by many fouls from the South Americans, Spain was able to come out with a 1-0 victory, ensuring they entered the knockout stages as group leaders and eliminating Uruguay along the way.
