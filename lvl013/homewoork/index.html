kirame




KIRAME is a rising Georgian hip-hop artist representing the new generation of the country’s rap scene,
known for his raw energy, authentic storytelling, and modern sound that blends trap influences with
emotional depth. His music reflects real-life experiences, ambition, struggle, and personal growth, creating a strong connection with listeners who relate to his
honest and unfiltered lyrics. KIRAME stands out through his unique style, combining powerful beats with melodic flows and a distinct voice
that captures both intensity and vulnerability. Inspired by street culture, youth energy, and global hip-hop trends, he continues to push boundaries and shape his own
identity within the Georgian music industry. With every release, KIRAME evolves as an artist, delivering tracks that are not only sonically engaging but also
meaningful, making him one of the promising names to watch in the growing Georgian hip-hop scene.
